THE MISSING LIST — TECHNICAL PLAN
A school-level dropout risk register for South Africa
=========================================================
This is the working plan: how the model gets built, validated and
defended. Problem and solution framing live in the project
specification document. Variable definitions live there too.


1. THE MODELLING POPULATION
---------------------------------------------------------
From 478,761 school-year records covering every school type, we
model only schools that can hold a Grade 10-12 cohort:

  SECONDARY and COMBINED phase     184,675 rows
                                    12,443 schools
                                    19 years, 2007-2025
                                    9 provinces, 196 districts

Primary and intermediate schools are excluded. A primary school
has no matric cohort, so it cannot contribute a single
observation to the question being asked. This is not a sampling
choice; it is the definition of the population.

Exclusions applied on top:
  - schools below a minimum enrolment, where one learner leaving
    moves the rate by several percentage points and the signal is
    swamped by noise
  - schools flagged as closing, which lose learners for reasons
    that have nothing to do with dropout risk
  - records where the identifier changed that year, because a
    merger looks exactly like severe attrition and is not

Each exclusion is recorded with the row count it costs, so the
population can be reconstructed and challenged.


2. THE TARGET VARIABLE
---------------------------------------------------------
What we would ideally measure is cohort survival: of the learners
who started Grade 10 at a school, how many reached matric. That
requires per-grade enrolment, which we do not have.

What we can measure is throughput:

  survival = matric candidates in year Y
             ---------------------------------------------
             expected cohort from enrolment in year Y

For a school spanning Grades 8-12, one grade is roughly a fifth
of total enrolment, so the expected cohort is enrolment / 5.

Tested on real data for one province: median 0.71, implying
roughly 29% attrition. Published research puts compounded
Grade 10-12 losses at around a third. The measure lands where
the literature says it should, which is the strongest validation
available without the gated data.

BINARY TARGET
The metrics this project reports — AUC, Gini, precision, recall,
F1 — require a binary outcome, and weight of evidence requires
one too. So survival is thresholded:

  is_high_risk = 1 where survival falls in the worst quintile
                   nationally
               = 0 otherwise

The threshold is set at the 20th percentile of the observed
distribution rather than at a round number. That makes it
answerable: "the worst-performing fifth of schools in the
country" is a policy-meaningful statement. "Below 0.6" is not.

WHAT THIS TARGET IS NOT
It is a throughput indicator, not a census of individual
learners. It conflates a genuine loss of learners with a
shrinking intake. Schools whose enrolment is falling for
demographic reasons will look worse than they are. This is
stated plainly wherever the number appears, and it is the single
largest limitation of the project.


3. TRAIN / TEST SPLIT
---------------------------------------------------------
SPLIT BY YEAR. NEVER AT RANDOM.

  train       2007 - 2020
  validate    2021 - 2022
  test        2023 - 2024

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS
The same school appears in up to nineteen rows, one per year. A
random split puts a school's 2019 row in training and its 2021
row in testing. The model then scores well by recognising the
school, not by learning anything that transfers to a school it
has never seen.

The failure is silent. Nothing errors. AUC comes back high. The
model is useless on any new school and nobody finds out until it
is deployed — or until a judge asks how the split was done.

A time split also matches how the model would actually be used:
trained on history, applied to a year that has not happened yet.

SECOND LEAKAGE GUARD
Weight-of-evidence bins are fitted on training years only, then
applied unchanged to validation and test. Fitting them across all
years lets every bin see the test set, which inflates every
metric downstream. fit() and transform() are separate functions
for exactly this reason and must not be collapsed.

THIRD GUARD
No feature may use information from the year it predicts. An
enrolment trend for year Y is built from years Y-1 and earlier.
Building it from Y+1 would be predicting the past from the
future.


4. FEATURE ENGINEERING
---------------------------------------------------------
Raw columns describe a school at a moment. Risk is about
direction and context, so most features are constructed.

FROM ENROLMENT HISTORY
  enrolment_change_1y      short-term movement
  enrolment_change_3y      the main trend signal
  enrolment_change_5y      long-run direction
  enrolment_volatility     standard deviation over time;
                           instability is a warning regardless of
                           direction
  size_vs_district         school size against its district
                           median; 200 learners means different
                           things in a metro and a rural district

FROM STAFFING
  learner_educator_ratio   crowding
  ratio_vs_province        the same, relative to provincial norm

  Note: learners and educators correlate at 0.89. Both in a
  linear model would split the coefficient unpredictably between
  them and make individual scores unexplainable. The ratio
  replaces both.

FROM FUNDING AND CONTEXT
  quintile                 1-5, the strongest expected predictor
  nofee                    checked against quintile for
                           redundancy before both are used
  urbanrural               with deep rural kept separate
  distance_to_urban        from coordinates; a measured proxy for
                           isolation rather than a category

FROM STRUCTURE
  school_age               years since first appearance
  renumber_flag            identifier changed; separates
                           administrative change from attrition

FROM MATRIC RESULTS
  matric_throughput        candidates against enrolment two years
                           earlier
  throughput_trend         direction over three years
  progressed_share         share advanced without meeting
                           requirements; a direct indicator of a
                           struggling pipeline
  pass_rate_trend          three-year direction

A MEASURED FINDING THAT SHAPES THIS
Learner:educator ratio by quintile: 29.5, 28.9, 30.2, 29.1, 22.2
for quintiles 1 to 5. Flat across quintiles 1-4, breaking only at
quintile 5. Class size is largely equalised by state staffing,
and the advantage at the top comes from schools funding
additional posts themselves. So ratio alone is a weak
discriminator between the poorer bands, and features relative to
provincial and district norms matter more than absolute ones.


5. MISSING DATA
---------------------------------------------------------
The treatment depends on whether a value is missing at random or
missing for a reason.

  educators (6.5% missing)
    Impute: median within province and year. Looks
    administrative. Median rather than mean because school sizes
    are heavily skewed. Grouped by province and year because a
    2009 and a 2024 school are not comparable.

  quintile (3.8% missing)
    Do not impute. Keep as its own level.
    Measured: schools with unknown quintile have a median of 296
    learners against 394-924 for known bands. They are
    systematically smaller. The absence is informative, and
    imputing it would erase a real category of small school.

  coordinates (0.3% missing, plus 364 at latitude zero)
    Reject rather than repair. Latitude zero is a point in the
    Atlantic Ocean. Distance features are simply unavailable for
    those records.

  target
    Never imputed. Imputing an outcome invents the answer the
    model is meant to learn.

An indicator column marks every imputed value, so a model can
learn from the fact of absence if it carries signal.


6. CATEGORICAL ENCODING AND BINNING
---------------------------------------------------------
ENCODING
  Low cardinality — province, quintile, urban/rural, fee status —
  weight of evidence, consistent with the scorecard.

  High cardinality — district, 195 values — also weight of
  evidence. One column per district would produce 195 mostly
  empty columns, and a tree would split on noise.

  UNKNOWN is a level everywhere, never dropped.

BINNING
  Numeric features are cut into quantile bins, starting at ten.
  Quantiles rather than equal width: school sizes are heavily
  skewed and equal-width bins would put almost everything in the
  first bucket.

  Bins are then merged until risk moves monotonically across
  them. A scorecard where the middle band is riskier than both
  extremes cannot be explained to the person acting on it, and an
  unexplainable scorecard defeats the purpose of choosing one.

  Minimum 5% of the population per bin. Below that a bin is
  noise.


7. MODELS
---------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY: logistic regression over weight-of-evidence bins
  A scorecard. Every score decomposes into visible weights, so
  any individual result can be explained line by line. This is
  the deliverable.

BENCHMARK: gradient boosting over the same features
  Not to ship, but to answer one question: how much accuracy does
  interpretability cost? If the gap is small, the scorecard is
  clearly right. If the gap is large, that is worth knowing and
  reporting honestly rather than hiding.

DECISION RULE
  Ship the interpretable model unless the benchmark beats it by a
  margin large enough to matter in practice. Interpretability is
  not a nice-to-have here: the output directs public spending,
  and a score that cannot be explained cannot be defended by the
  person who has to act on it.


8. EVALUATION
---------------------------------------------------------
REPORTED ON THE TEST YEARS ONLY

  AUC          ranking quality, which is what this problem is
  Gini         = 2 x AUC - 1; the same information, reported
               because it is conventional in scorecards
  Precision    of schools flagged, how many were genuinely at
               risk
  Recall       of schools genuinely at risk, how many we caught
  F1           where a single balanced number is needed
  Accuracy     reported, never led with — on an imbalanced target
               it flatters a model that has learned nothing

WHICH ERROR MATTERS MORE
A false positive sends support to a school that needed less than
predicted. A false negative leaves a school losing learners
unflagged. The second is worse, so recall is favoured over
precision, and the threshold is tuned accordingly. This is a
stated policy choice, not a statistical default.

ALSO REPORTED
  Information value per variable, before modelling, to justify
  what entered the model.
  Score distribution across provinces, to check the model has not
  simply learned to rank provinces.
  Calibration: among schools scored at a given risk, how many
  were actually at risk.


9. WHAT COULD GO WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------
  Target conflates shrinking intake with genuine attrition.
    Mitigation: report enrolment trend alongside every score so a
    user can see which is happening. Stated as a limitation
    wherever the number appears.

  The model learns province rather than risk.
    Mitigation: check score distribution by province; build
    features relative to provincial norms rather than absolute.

  An information value above 0.5 on any variable.
    Treat as a leakage warning, not a success. Usually means the
    variable encodes the answer rather than predicting it.

  Quintile dominates and the model becomes a funding lookup.
    Mitigation: report performance with and without quintile. If
    it adds nothing beyond quintile, that is a finding worth
    stating honestly rather than concealing.

  Coverage gaps by province-year.
    One province is missing its most recent year at source.
    Handled explicitly rather than allowed to read as a province
    that stopped existing.


10. SEQUENCE
---------------------------------------------------------
  1  Extract matric results from the report PDFs into a table
     keyed on school and year.                        BLOCKING
  2  Join to the school panel on the normalised identifier.
  3  Build the target; inspect its distribution; set the
     threshold from the data and have it approved.
  4  Build features; compute information value for each; drop
     what does not earn a place.
  5  Split by year. Fit bins on training years only.
  6  Train the scorecard. Train the benchmark. Compare.
  7  Evaluate on test years. Produce the ranked register.
  8  Surface results in the app: metrics, feature importance,
     and the ranked list itself.

Step 1 blocks everything after it. Nothing downstream can start
until the matric figures exist as a table.
