DATA DICTIONARY — EVERY COLUMN, WHAT IT MEANS, HOW IT IS TREATED
The Missing List · DataQuest · Theme: Education
=========================================================

Fill rates are measured, not estimated. Where a column is dirty, the number
of distinct values is given against the number it should have - that gap is
the number of spellings that need mapping before the column is usable.


TABLE 1 — school_year_panel.csv.gz
478,761 rows · 20 columns · 29,655 schools · 2007-2025 · all 9 provinces
This is the raw joined panel. It is deliberately uncleaned.
---------------------------------------------------------

IDENTITY AND STRUCTURE

  emis            100%   29,655 distinct
    The national school identifier, and the join key for everything. Published
    with and without a leading zero, so it is normalised to bare digits before
    any join. Get this wrong and joins fail silently rather than loudly.

  year            100%   19 distinct
    Data year, 2007-2025. Used for the train/test split. Never a predictor -
    a model that learns "2019 was bad" has learned nothing transferable.

  school           99.9%  55,214 distinct
    School name. Not modelled. Kept so a ranked result is readable by a human
    who has to go and visit it. More distinct values than schools because
    names are spelled inconsistently across years.

  province        100%   9 distinct
    Clean after normalisation. Was 11 before: KwaZulu-Natal is published as
    both KZ and KZN, Gauteng as both GT and GP.

  district        100%   222 distinct
    Education district. High cardinality, so encoded by weight of evidence
    rather than one column per district.

  circuit          82.8%  1,782 distinct
    Sub-district. Too sparse and too fine to be reliable. Excluded.

  source_file     100%   140 distinct
    Which spreadsheet a row came from. Not modelled; kept so any figure can be
    traced back to the published file it came from.

CLASSIFICATION

  phase           100%   21 distinct, should be ~6
    The grade range a school covers. Appears as SECONDARY SCHOOL, Secondary
    School, Secondary and SECONDARY, among others. This is how the modelling
    population is identified, so it has to be right - and 8,792 schools change
    their recorded phase between years, which is why the label alone cannot be
    trusted to filter on.

  sector          100%   6 distinct, should be 2
    Public or independent. One of the six values is ORDINARY, which is a
    school type and not a sector at all.

  type            100%   5 distinct
    Institution type. Almost entirely one value once normalised, so it carries
    no information and is not modelled.

  status          100%   10 distinct, should be ~3
    OPEN, Open and OPERATIONAL all mean the same thing. Used to tell a school
    that is closing from one that is failing - they lose learners for entirely
    different reasons.

FUNDING AND CONTEXT

  quintile         97.3%  17 distinct, should be 5
    The funding band, 1 poorest to 5 wealthiest. Written as 3, Q3 and NQ3 in
    different years, plus TO BE UPDATED and NOT APPLICABLE. Normalised to 1-5
    plus unknown.

    Unknown is kept as its own level rather than imputed. Measured, not
    assumed: schools with no recorded quintile have a median of 296 learners
    against 394-924 for known bands. They are systematically smaller, so the
    absence is itself informative and imputing it would erase a real category.

  nofee            89.2%  11 distinct, should be 2
    Whether the school charges fees. Values include YES, No Fee, NO, Fee
    Charging, Fee Paying and Free Charging - the last being a typo at source.
    Correlates 0.85 with quintile, so only one of the two enters the model.

  urbanrural       99.6%  12 distinct, should be ~4
    Settlement type. DEEP RURAL is kept separate from RURAL, because the
    source distinguishes them deliberately.

  lat, lon         99.5%
    Coordinates. 724 records sit at latitude or longitude zero - a point in
    the Atlantic Ocean roughly 2,000km offshore, not a school. Those are
    rejected rather than mapped, since any distance built on them is wrong.
    A further 27,403 fall outside South Africa's latitude range entirely.

SIZE AND STAFFING

  learners        100%
    Enrolment that year. The basis of school size and every trend measure.
    Complete throughout, which is what makes it usable as a denominator.

  educators        94.0%
    Teaching staff. Missing on 6% of rows, imputed by provincial median within
    the same year - a 2009 school and a 2024 school are not comparable.

  old_emis, new_emis   83.5%, 79.7%
    Renumbering history. Used to detect mergers: a merged school loses
    learners for administrative reasons and would otherwise read as severe
    attrition.

  province        100%    province
  district          4.8%  printed once per block in the source and left blank

TABLE 3 — model_frame.csv.gz
146,944 rows · 47 columns
The clean panel plus features and target. This is what the model trains on.
---------------------------------------------------------

THE TARGET

  loss            13.2% positive
    1 where a school's enrolment over the next two years falls at least 15
    percentage points more than its provincial median, for schools of 100+
    learners.

    Measured against the province, not in absolute terms, so a provincial
    policy shift is not read as thousands of schools failing at once.

    WHAT IT IS NOT: dropout. A learner who transfers to a better school and
    one who leaves education entirely look identical in a headcount. The
    honest claim is that these schools are shedding learners faster than their
    province, not that those learners have left the system.

  split
    train 73,105 · valid 16,398 · test 24,891 · score 8,154 · unused 24,396
    Split by year, never at random. A school appears in up to nineteen rows,
    so a random split would train and test on the same school and the model
    would score well by recognising it.

DERIVED FROM ENROLMENT HISTORY

  log_learners            school size, logged because the distribution is
                          heavily skewed
  chg1, chg3              enrolment change over one and three years
  chg1_rel_prov           the same, measured against the province
  vol                     enrolment volatility; instability is a warning
                          independent of direction
  peak_ratio              enrolment as a share of the school's own peak
  years_observed          how long the school has been in the record

DERIVED FROM STAFFING

  ler                     learners per educator. A ratio rather than both
                          counts, because the two correlate at 0.89 and
                          including both makes an individual score
                          unexplainable
  edu_chg1                educator change over the last year

DERIVED FROM CONTEXT

  district_chg1           the district's typical enrolment change
  district_schools        how many secondary schools the district holds
  nearby_5km, nearby_10km schools within reach
  nearby_chg1             what those neighbours are doing
  size_vs_nearby          size relative to the schools around it

DERIVED FROM MATRIC RESULTS

  throughput              matric candidates as a share of the school
  throughput_chg1/chg3    how that share is moving
  throughput_vol          how erratic it has been
  wrote_chg1, wrote_chg3  change in the size of the matric class
  pass_rate               matric pass rate — the strongest single variable in
                          the final model
  pass_rate_chg1          whether results are improving or collapsing
  progressed_share        share of the matric class advanced without meeting
                          requirements
  matric_age              how old the school's latest matric record is, so a
                          stale figure can be discounted


HOW MISSING DATA IS TREATED
---------------------------------------------------------

The treatment depends entirely on whether a value is missing at random or
missing for a reason. Getting this wrong erases signal rather than repairing
it.

  educators       impute, by provincial median within the year. The gap looks
                  administrative. Median rather than mean, because school
                  sizes are heavily skewed.

  quintile        keep unknown as its own level. Measured above: those schools
                  are systematically smaller, so the absence carries
                  information.

  coordinates     reject. A location cannot be invented, so distance features
                  are simply unavailable for those rows.

  matric          carry forward with an age marker, never backward. Forward is
                  what a district official would already have on file; backward
                  would be reading the future.

  target          never impute. Imputing an outcome invents the answer the
                  model is meant to learn.


HOW CATEGORICAL VARIABLES ARE ENCODED
---------------------------------------------------------

  Low cardinality  province, quintile, urban/rural, fee status — weight of
                   evidence, consistent with the scorecard.

  High cardinality district, 222 values — also weight of evidence. One column
                   per district would produce 222 mostly empty columns and a
                   model that splits on noise.

  Unknown          preserved as a category everywhere, never dropped.

  Binning          numeric variables are cut into bins and the bins merged
                   until risk moves in one direction across them. A scorecard
                   where the middle band is riskier than both extremes cannot
                   be explained to the person acting on it. Each bin holds at
                   least 5% of the population; below that it is noise.
